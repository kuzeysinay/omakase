Oppenheimer and the Physical Gravity of IMAX 70mm

READY
Christopher Nolan commissioned Kodak to engineer 65mm IMAX black-and-white film for the very first time in cinema history. As the projector spooled the 11-mile, 600-pound platter, the mechanical whirr on set was as hypnotic as the imagery. For Nolan, this was not mere nostalgia; it was an uncompromising quest for tangible optical presence.
Source: Kodak Motion Picture Film Archives; Hoyte van Hoytema interviews

Our Philosophy

Trust the Chef

In Japanese dining, 'Omakase' means leaving the menu to the chef. We replaced the endless scroll with focused, thoughtful daily posts.

Algorithmic Endless Stream
  • — Addictive, insatiable scrolling loops (doomscrolling)
  • — Outrage-driven, engagement-baiting headlines
  • — Ads, sponsored clutter, and visual noise
  • — Mental fatigue and emptiness after closing the app
The Omakase Approach
  • + Mindful 15–25s reading cadence that respects comprehension
  • + Verified, timeless micro-stories across 12 cultural domains
  • + Zero ads, pure monochrome ink-on-paper serenity
  • + Lasting curiosity and mental clarity after every session

Frequently Asked Questions

Omakase is a native iOS app that transforms your chosen hyper-specific subtopics across 12 cultural disciplines, custom topics, or your Letterboxd diary into verified, real-time micro-stories powered by AI.
Conventional social feeds condition users to swipe in fractions of a second without absorbing anything. Omakase enforces a 15–25 second digestion window so every story is genuinely read.
Our backend uses strict factual grounding rules and a multi-layer moderation pipeline to prevent fabrication, prioritizing documented historical records, peer-reviewed research, and primary sources.
Simply enter your Letterboxd handle in the Film Bento card—no password required. Omakase reads your public RSS diary of 50 recent films and serves verified behind-the-scenes fun-facts (1/day free, unlimited with Pro).
Share favorite stories to the Community timeline under your @username handle, export monochrome getomakase.app cards in Story (9:16), Post (4:5), or Square (1:1) formats to Instagram, WhatsApp & X, or bookmark posts into 12 offline collections.
Omakase displays zero ads, performs zero cross-app tracking (IDFA), and never sells your data. It supports Sign in with Apple and includes one-tap permanent Account Deletion directly in the Profile tab.
